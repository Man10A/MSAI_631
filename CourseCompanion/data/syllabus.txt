COURSE DESCRIPTION
This course examines the convergence of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) and how they revolutionize the way humans interact with machines and digital systems. The course emphasizes the symbiotic relationship between AI and HCI and prepares students to design, develop, and evaluate intelligent interactive systems.

COURSE OBJECTIVES
Upon completion of this course, students will:
Understand foundational concepts of AI and HCI and how their integration shapes modern interactive systems.
Apply user-centered design principles, usability heuristics, and accessibility guidelines.
Integrate machine learning techniques such as classification, clustering, and pattern recognition into adaptive interfaces.
Develop AI-powered natural language interfaces including conversational agents and chatbots.
Identify ethical challenges including bias, privacy, transparency, and accountability in AI systems.
Design, prototype, and evaluate AI-driven interactive systems using usability testing and iterative refinement.

LEARNER OUTCOMES
Students will be able to:
Analyze AI and HCI and their interaction.
Analyze multimodal interfaces.
Evaluate user-centric design principles.
Create and discuss usability heuristics.
Evaluate accessibility guidelines and their legal and ethical implications.
Evaluate user-friendliness of applications.
Apply AI techniques to improve user experience.
Create a recommendation system using AI and HCI concepts.
Apply NLP concepts such as tokenization in a chatbot.
Evaluate prompt engineering techniques.
Create an intelligent chatbot integrating a traditional chatbot with AI-as-a-Service (e.g., Azure Cognitive Services).
