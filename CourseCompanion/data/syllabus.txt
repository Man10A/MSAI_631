Course: MSAI-631 Artificial Intelligence for Human-Computer Interaction

This course explores how AI and HCI work together to create better interactive systems.
Topics include design thinking, human-centered AI, conversational agents, multimodal
interfaces, and evaluation methods.

The course runs for a full term with weekly lectures, discussions, and assignments.
Students are expected to complete readings, individual homework, and at least one
group project involving an AI-based interactive system.

Grading policy (example for the bot, not the real syllabus):
- Homework and quizzes: 25%
- Group project: 35%
- Midterm and final assessments: 30%
- Participation and attendance: 10%

Late submission policy (example for the bot):
Assignments submitted up to 24 hours late incur a 10% penalty on the earned score.
Assignments submitted more than 24 hours late may receive a zero unless prior approval
has been obtained from the instructor.

Office hours:
The instructor holds weekly office hours as listed in the official syllabus. Students
should email the instructor to confirm times or request an appointment.
